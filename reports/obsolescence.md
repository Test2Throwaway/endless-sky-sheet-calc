# Smart obsolescence: power and cooling

Which generators and coolers a designer ever needs. Generated by `python -m sheetcalc`.

1. **Dominated:** another outfit is no bigger and no worse on any stat.
2. **Off the ladder:** a passive cooler that never appears on the exact passive-cooling ladder.
3. **Never in a best plant / replaceable:** judged by the joint power plant (generators, passive
   and active cooling together) at every load in the grid below. *Never in a best plant* means it
   is not part of the least-space plant at any of them; *replaceable* means it sometimes is, but
   banning it never costs any space (ties go to the outfit that is easier to get).

Loads tested: energy draw 0–3800/s × heat 0–15000/s (27 × 17 points), active cooling counted at 75% heat.
