# Power plant: generators + passive + active cooling

Least total outfit space of generators, passive coolers and active coolers that covers a given
energy draw and heat load from the rest of the ship. All three are searched together, so active
coolers' energy and the generators' heat are both accounted for without iterating.
Active cooling is counted at 75% heat (setting `ACTIVE_COOLING_HEAT_FRACTION`).
Generated by `python -m sheetcalc`.
