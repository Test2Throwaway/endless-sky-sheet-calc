# Legacy

`Ship_designer_master_sheet.xlsx` is the original ship designer spreadsheet, put together
partly by hand, preserved here as-is for reference.

Sheets: Drives, Jammers, Fuel, master sheet (glossary of stat names), Scanners,
Generators, Batteries, Cooling, Engines, Shields, Cargo & Outfit Expansions,
Anti-Missile, several Utility Weapons sheets (shield disabling, moving, slowing,
shield disruption, tractor beam, heat damage, ion), and Damage Weapons.
